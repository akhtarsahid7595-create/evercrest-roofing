## Competitor research recovered

The repeatedly referenced competitor is **D. Hennessy Roofing**, described as having been online since 2018 and possessing greater website history and authority.

The useful lessons taken from the comparison were:

- Established roofing sites explain services in more depth.
- Helpful customer questions improve usefulness and search relevance.
- Stronger internal content structure supports discovery.
- Evercrest should use the competitor as a benchmark, not copy its content.
- Only verified Evercrest services, locations, claims, reviews, and project information should be published.

## Proposed Phase 5 — Authority, proof, and internal structure

Phase 5 should begin only after confirming Phase 3 and Phase 4 deliverables and checking that the Phase 2 pages are indexed and performing normally.

### 1. Establish the baseline

- Export the last 28–90 days from Google Search Console.
- Record indexed URLs, queries, impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position.
- Record calls, quote submissions, and key service-page engagement where analytics are available.
- Identify pages with impressions but weak CTR and pages ranking on positions 8–30.

### 2. Add genuine proof to priority pages

Use only client-approved material:

- Real project descriptions with problem, work completed, and outcome.
- Before-and-after photographs with descriptive filenames and alt text.
- Client-approved testimonials with attribution that is accurate and permitted.
- Relevant internal links from proof sections to the matching service page.

Do not invent project results, review scores, guarantees, certifications, or locations.

### 3. Improve VELUX information

- Clarify the exact VELUX-related services Evercrest provides.
- Explain common customer problems and assessment considerations.
- Add a small FAQ section based on real customer questions.
- Link the page to relevant roof-repair, flat-roof, and emergency-repair pages where appropriate.

### 4. Strengthen internal linking

Create a deliberate, natural network between related existing pages:

- Roof Repairs ↔ Emergency Roof Repairs
- Roof Repairs ↔ Flat Roofing
- Chimney Repairs ↔ Roof Repairs
- Gutter Repairs ↔ Gutter Cleaning
- Relevant project examples ↔ the service delivered

Use descriptive anchor text, avoid repetitive exact-match anchors, and ensure every important page is reachable through the site navigation or contextual links.

### 5. Publish one carefully selected guide

Choose the guide only after Search Console and customer questions identify a real information gap. Candidate themes include:

- What to do when a roof starts leaking
- Roof repair or replacement: what to check first
- How to tell whether gutters need cleaning or repair

The guide should support commercial pages, answer a genuine customer need, and link to relevant services. Avoid publishing a batch of low-value posts.

### 6. Local trust and citations

- Audit the Google Business Profile and core business details.
- Keep name, address, phone, service area, opening hours, and website details consistent.
- Seek genuine customer reviews through normal business processes.
- Add only relevant, legitimate local or trade citations.
- Do not create fake locations or location pages for areas not genuinely served.

### 7. Technical and structured-data check

- Confirm indexability, canonical URLs, XML sitemap coverage, robots rules, mobile layout, and page speed.
- Validate the page headings, titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, and internal links.
- Add structured data only where it accurately represents the page and complies with Google’s guidelines.

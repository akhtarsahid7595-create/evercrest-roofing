## Main opportunity against the competitor

The competitor has breadth, but its organic footprint is shallow: the homepage carries most of its estimated traffic, while many service and information terms rank between positions 15 and 79. Evercrest can compete by building a clearer service architecture with stronger, genuinely useful pages rather than copying the competitor's location-page volume.

## Phase 1 decisions

- Do not create pages for every competitor URL.
- Do not create duplicate pages for the same search intent.
- Do not create mass Leinster location pages yet.
- Do not publish unsupported pricing, guarantees, certifications, reviews, or project claims.
- Do not change the completed Phase 2 pages unnecessarily; first measure and strengthen their internal links.
- Use one page per distinct commercial intent and one guide per distinct informational intent.
